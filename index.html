About Lumora Academy

Lumora Academy was founded to provide equitable access to STEM opportunities for students from underrepresented and low-income backgrounds.

Our Values:

  • 100% Free Programs
  • Equity-Focused Initiatives
  • Student Leadership & Mentorship
  • Global Impact

Programs We Offer

  • STEM Workshops: Hands-on coding, robotics, and engineering workshops.
  • Mentorship: Guidance from experienced STEM students and professionals.
  • Tutoring: One-on-one support in math, science, and programming.
  • Scholarships & Opportunities: Access to internships, research, and scholarships.
