Breaking Barriers. Creating Opportunities. Changing Lives.

Lumora Academy was founded on a simple belief: every student deserves the chance to pursue their STEM dreams, regardless of zip code, family income, or background.

We know that underrepresented and low-income students face real barriers—lack of resources, limited access to mentors, and fewer opportunities. That's why everything we offer is completely free and designed to level the playing field.

Our student-led chapters provide hands-on workshops, college prep, scholarship guidance, and a supportive community that says: "Yes, you belong in STEM. Yes, you can achieve great things."
